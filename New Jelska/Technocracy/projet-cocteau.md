# Projet Cocteau

## Internal Concept Document (Universe Canon)

**Classification:** Très Secret -- COCTEAU\
**Nature:** Informal Franco-Polish Technocratic Programme\
**Established:** Early 1980s

### Overview

Projet Cocteau is a clandestine, long-term initiative created within the
Franco-Polish Technocracy to identify, protect and cultivate
exceptionally gifted children whose future contributions are judged to
be of strategic importance.

Its purpose is not to manufacture genius, but to ensure that naturally
extraordinary individuals are never lost through neglect, politics,
poverty or institutional failure.

The programme takes its name from Jean Cocteau's *Les Enfants
Terribles*.

## Philosophy

> Civilizations have obligations toward exceptional children.

The programme rejects both treating gifted children as state property
and leaving their development entirely to chance.

Its informal maxim is:

> Protect. Never possess.

## Enfant d'État

Within the innermost circle, selected individuals are referred to as
**Enfants d'État**.

This is not a legal status but an internal honorific reserved for those
whose development is considered strategically important.

## Selection

Candidates are identified through long-term observation rather than
single examinations.

Indicators include:

-   scientific originality
-   mathematical ability
-   leadership
-   strategic reasoning
-   emotional resilience
-   linguistic aptitude
-   ethical maturity

## Support

Support is intentionally subtle:

-   scholarships
-   mentors
-   access to elite education
-   research opportunities
-   language education
-   discreet protection
-   introductions to strategic institutions

Children are encouraged, never coerced.

## Organization

The programme belongs to the informal Franco-Polish Technocracy rather
than any formal ministry.

France provides most institutional capacity.

Poland primarily contributes identification, protection and
coordination.

## Compartmentalization (1994)

### France

Only a handful of senior officials know the programme in full.

### Poland

Knowledge is limited to:

-   Chief of the civilian intelligence service
-   Secretary of the KBP
-   Director of the Polish Cocteau Cell
-   Krzysztof Bryne-Jelski (co-founder)

Neither the President nor the sitting Prime Minister are routinely
informed.

Military leadership is not briefed.

## Scale (1994)

-   thousands screened
-   hundreds observed
-   dozens actively supported
-   roughly fifteen recognized internally as Enfants d'État

## Ethical Principles

Following the Król Incident (1994), the programme adopts stricter
internal practice:

-   avoid intrusive observation of minors whenever possible
-   parents should be approached before direct observation where
    feasible
-   Enfants d'État are never intelligence assets
-   stewardship must never become exploitation

## Notable Individuals

### Antonina Jelska

Exceptional scientist whose later work produces the Jelska Process, ASP
and ATHENA.

### Maksymilian Król

Identified for strategic reasoning, leadership and languages; later
becomes Chief of Polish Military Intelligence.

## Founders' Principle

> We do not create extraordinary people.
>
> We merely ensure that the world does not prevent them from becoming
> who they already could become.
