## Physical Presence

**Height:** 187 cm. Dark brown hair, clean-cut, broad-shouldered. In photographs from this period, he is rarely looking at the camera — he is looking at whatever he is pointing at. A man constitutionally oriented toward the horizon.

**Bearing:** Still, economical, unhurried. Not the physical presence of a soldier (though he did the Polytechnique military year) — more precisely the presence of a man who has already decided what is going to happen and is mildly patient while reality catches up. He does not enter rooms dramatically. He enters rooms early and is already reading something by the time others arrive.

**Dress:** Conservative, well-cut, European. Not showy. At 33, already wearing the suit of a man a decade older — which in Polish political context in 1991 is an asset, not a liability.

**Resemblance:** Antonina's grey-blue eyes and facial structure come from him. People who know both will clock it immediately.

## Education

### Polish schools in Norway

Raised in Stavanger by a Polish mother who taught him Polish at home and enrolled him in the Polish diaspora school network. He grew up bilingual Polish-Norwegian, educated in Polish tradition while physically in Norway. Did not visit Poland until 1989.

### Lycée Sainte-Geneviève (Ginette), Versailles — CPGE

*~1976–1978, ages 18–20*

"Ginette" is one of the two or three CPGE institutions in France whose primary institutional purpose is to produce Polytechnique admissions. A Norwegian-Polish boy doing CPGE at Ginette in 1976 was not a coincidence. Whether Operation Świder's French handlers cultivated him into this path or he arrived there independently, the result is that by age 20 he was already inside the formation pipeline of the French governing class.

### École Polytechnique — Corps des Mines

*~1978–1982, ages 20–24*

**The school:** L'X. France's apex scientific and technical grande école, founded by the Revolution, permanently indebted to Napoleon, historically producing more heads of state and national industrial leaders than any comparable institution in the world.

**The corps:** X-Mines. The Corps des Mines is the most prestigious of the French grands corps — the institutional backbone of French dirigisme, producing ministers, PDGs of national champions, heads of energy policy, regulatory architecture, and industrial strategy. X-Mines officers understand, by formation and by network, how a state builds and protects its economic sovereignty. This is not incidental to what Krzysztof is sent to do in Poland. It is the precise qualification required.

**The military year:** As a polytechnicien, he completed the formation militaire — the military year embedded in the l'X curriculum. As a foreign-born officer cadet, this placed him inside the French military structure at a formative age, building institutional relationships that would underpin the DGSE connection throughout his operational career.

**The network:** The *camarades* (the informal brotherhood of all X graduates) is one of the densest and most powerful alumni networks in the world. Krzysztof graduates with access to people who will, within a decade, run France's energy companies, sit in ministerial cabinets, and direct the DGSE. He is not a peripheral asset. He is part of the inner architecture.

*Note: Antonina will follow him to l'X two decades later. He will send her there deliberately, handing her his own network pre-loaded.*

### Université d'Amsterdam — Master's in Public Security

*~1982–1984*

Following a gap period (see below), he completed a master's in public security at the University of Amsterdam. The degree is genuine — it is also the credential that makes "offshore security consultant at Shell" a plausible career entry. The Amsterdam posting was pre-arranged as the operational base for the next phase of his work.

## Intelligence Formation & Operation Świder

### First contact — 1975 (age 17)

His father's tunnel-drilling company received a US military contract for renovating a Norwegian base. Krzysztof, through his father's business, was first exposed to uniformed services contexts at 17. He was noticed. The notice was passed upward through channels that would eventually connect to the CIA faction running the early phases of what would become Operation Świder.

### The gap year — ~1982

Following Polytechnique, he took what was officially described as a year of rest between his degrees. In practice: a short intelligence sortie under Norwegian direction (E-tjenesten), his first operational deployment. Norway's intelligence service had specific interests in Polish affairs — significant diaspora, North Sea financial flows, proximity to the Soviet northern fleet — and served as Krzysztof's initial handler before he was passed to the CIA/DGSE bilateral channel. He is one of the vanishingly few assets who was cultivated across three intelligence services sequentially before being handed to a fourth.

### Operation Foreuse / Świder — Architecture

**Codename:** The operation carries a single name pronounced three ways:

- **Świder** (Polish) — a drill. Named after a classified document hidden by Marian Spychalski (Polish head of state, 1968–70), concerning natural resources in the Suwałki region — concealed from Moscow to prevent Soviet exploitation. The Poles named it. The Technocracy adopted it.
- **Foreuse** (French) — a tunnel boring machine. A private joke: Krzysztof's father ran a tunnel-drilling company. The operation is named after the tool his family manufactured. Krzysztof IS the foreuse. That he is also Corps des Mines completes the metaphor.
- **"For Use"** (English) — the anglophone pronunciation. Poland is being made *for use*.

**Strategic purpose:** To liberate Iron Curtain countries from Soviet influence and place them in the Western sphere — but specifically, in the French conception, into a Western sphere defined by European sovereignty rather than American hegemony. This is the Gaullist dimension the CIA faction running the operation did not fully advertise to Washington.

**Primary architect:** François Mitterrand, personally. Operation Świder is the DGSE's primary institutional focus in this period — not a sideshow but the main effort. French operatives are embedded throughout the operation's scope, training and "training" Polish counterparts, facilitating the NATOisation and francisation of Polish military structures.

**The CIA dimension:** A senior faction within the CIA co-architects the operation, operating in partial concealment from the official American administration. Their Gaullism is strategic, not ideological — they believe a strong, sovereign Europe is the best long-term bulwark against Soviet expansion, even if that means a Europe not fully subordinate to Washington. The operation began under official sanction in the mid-Cold War period and drifted into unauthorized Gaullist territory under Reagan/Casey. By 1991, the shape of what it is building — a Paris-Warsaw axis, not a Washington-Warsaw axis — is far enough from official American policy that it could not survive full scrutiny.

**Multinational participants (compartmentalized):**

| Service | Country | What they knew | Lane |
|---|---|---|---|
| DGSE | France | Full picture | Primary architect |
| CIA faction | USA | Full picture | Co-architect, deniable |
| E-tjenesten/PST | Norway | Own lane only | North Sea intel, Stavanger node |
| BVD (predecessor to AIVD) | Netherlands | Own lane only | Amsterdam financial infrastructure |
| SGRS (predecessor) | Belgium | Own lane only | NATO-adjacent, Brussels diplomatic |

The Norwegians, Dutch, and Belgians did not know about each other, did not know about the French Gaullist dimension, and in some cases did not know they were participating in a French-architected operation at all. Only Krzysztof and a handful of DGSE/CIA principals see the whole map.

**Polish interior contacts:** Tadeusz Syrop — ex-AWO, ex-WSW (Wojskowa Służba Wewnętrzna, communist military counterintelligence), currently a senior official in the First Department of the Polish Interior Ministry. Krzysztof's man inside the apparatus. The WSW knew where every body was buried in the Polish military. A man who switched sides from within it is worth ten recruited outside assets. His loyalty to Krzysztof and the operation predates 1989. He will be instrumental in designing the new uniformed services architecture.

### Krzysztof's specific operational role at Shell

*Royal Dutch Shell, Amsterdam, ~1984–1991 as "offshore extraction security consultant"*

**Cover functions (genuine):** Visiting oil rigs, maritime data analysis, security assessments of extraction infrastructure. The job is real; he does it competently.

**Operational functions:**

- **Network building:** Cultivating relationships with Polish technical specialists who traveled West for petroleum-related work (real phenomenon — Polish engineers worked with Western companies throughout the 1980s). These relationships become assets after 1989.
- **Financial facilitation:** Moving operational funds through oil-industry channels. Shell's international financial infrastructure provides cover for large transfers with plausible business justification.
- **Intelligence collection:** North Sea is a theater of Soviet maritime interest. His position provides legitimate access to maritime pattern-of-life data useful to both Norwegian and CIA partners.
- **Communication facilitation:** Cutout and relay functions between Warsaw-based Polish contacts and Western handlers. His multilingualism (Polish, Norwegian, French, Dutch, English) and professional mobility make him ideal.

## Katarzyna: The Operational Partnership

Katarzyna Laband was Dutch-born Polish — a scholar of ancient anthropology, Aramaic and Sumerian translation, already an émigré in Amsterdam when she and Krzysztof were introduced through the operation. She is not a peripheral spouse. She is a working part of the architecture.

Their relationship began as professional — two Polish émigrés, both operational assets, working adjacent lanes in Amsterdam. It became personal. By 1987, Antonina was born.

**What Katarzyna knows:** Everything, or close to it. She is fully in confidence. The marriage is a partnership of equals who happen to also be in love and raising children while running a long-term intelligence operation. She is not merely "the wife who knows" — she is a participant.

**Her academic role in the operation:** Her specialization in ancient anthropology and ancient languages has provided genuine academic cover for a range of activities. She has been an external consultant on exhumations and archaeological examinations — her credentials are real and respected, which makes her presence at sensitive sites unremarkable to outsiders.

*Note on the Jadwiga conspiracy theory: The 1987 examination of Jadwiga of Poland's remains (for the beatification process, canonized 1997) involved an academic team that included external consultants. Katarzyna was present in a consulting capacity. Some biological material from the examination was logged inconsistently and never publicly accounted for. Antonina was born nine months after the examination period. None of this constitutes evidence of anything. All of it is documented.*

## Return to Poland — October 1990

### Financial position

Krzysztof arrives in Warsaw as a multi-millionaire. Sources:

- Norwegian family inheritance (primarily his father's, whose tunnel-drilling company was eventually sold to Herrenknecht AG for ~$150 million)
- Personal investments and equity accumulated during the Shell years
- He is, in short, a returning émigré who does not need money and does not need to ask permission of anyone

### The residence

A former military officer's villa in Rembertów, Warsaw — purchased from the State Treasury's sale of ex-communist assets. Rembertów is chosen deliberately: proximity to the military installations there (military academy, training centers). He wants to be near the architecture he is going to reshape.

The villa itself is in good condition — maintained better than many comparable properties — and has been upgraded. It is large enough for a family with three young children and live-in domestic staff, functional rather than ostentatious. He doesn't buy it for prestige. He buys it for position.

### Official cover

A returning émigré who wants to try his hand in politics. This is essentially true. The Sejm is his entry point; ministerial office is the goal. He has the wealth to fund his own entry, the credentials (X-Mines, UvA, Shell) to be taken seriously, and — crucially — the knowledge of how mature democracies function that virtually no other candidate in 1991 Poland possesses. In a political landscape where everyone is improvising, a man who has watched France and the Netherlands and Norway operate at close range for fifteen years is operating on a completely different level of information.

## Character Profile

### Core disposition

Forward-facing. He is interested in the horizon, not in how he looks. The photograph captures this: he is pointing at something off-frame, not performing for the camera.

This manifests in every domain:

- **Professionally:** He thinks in years and decades. Every political move is evaluated against a long game most of his contemporaries can't see.
- **Intellectually:** He consumes information voraciously and is genuinely curious — not performatively curious, genuinely uncertain about things and wanting to know them.
- **Domestically:** He is mostly absent. Not coldly so, but absent in the way that a man with a ten-year construction project in his head is absent. He does not attend to what is directly around him.

### His relationship with politics

He enjoys it. He is good at it. The enjoyment and the competence are connected: he understands political dynamics through the lens of someone who has watched France, the Netherlands, and Norway operate for fifteen years, giving him an advantage in the political arms race that is simply informational. He knows tricks his opponents will not invent for another decade. He is not smarter than everyone — he is *better informed* than everyone, and in 1991 Polish politics, that is a decisive asymmetry.

### His relationship with intelligence work

Natural, not reluctant. He is not a man who does espionage because he was recruited and had no choice — he was recognized early because he had the instincts for it. He is comfortable with moral complexity, comfortable with long timelines, comfortable with the gap between his public reasons and his actual reasons. He does not experience this as hypocrisy. He experiences it as professionalism.

### His relationship with his children — January 1991

Distant by circumstance, not by inclination. He is consumed by the governmental work. He is building something that requires his full attention at precisely the period when his children are youngest and most need a present father. He will, per his own understanding, make up for this comprehensively after he retires. He is probably wrong about how easy that will be.

**Exception: Antonina.** He notices what she is immediately and cannot look away. He gives her tasks — algebra, logic problems, adult-level challenges — not as calculated grooming but out of his own curiosity and an instinctive understanding that starving an intelligence like hers would be worse than feeding it. He is afraid of what she is. He feeds it anyway. This is probably the most honest thing he does in this period.

## Position as of June 1991

Krzysztof Bryne-Jelski is 33 years old. He has been an intelligence operative for fifteen years, a Shell security consultant for seven, a husband for four, a father for three. He is now the Prime Minister of the Republic of Poland, sworn in on 25 June 1991. He has more money than he needs, more connections than anyone in Warsaw knows about, and a plan for Poland that is being actively implemented through his new cabinet.

He has power now. The elections are won, the cabinet is sworn in, and the 100 Days Plan is underway.

---

## Position as of May 1993 (Political Retirement)

At 35 years old, following the 1993 parliamentary elections and the swearing-in of Fryderyk Wagner's cabinet, Krzysztof Bryne-Jelski has stepped down as Prime Minister and resigned from the leadership of the PNP. He is officially on "political retirement." To the public, he is the architect who built the foundations of modern Poland and stepped aside. To the intelligence community, he is transitioning to the next phase of Operation Świder. He no longer needs the formal title of Prime Minister because the technocratic institutions he built (KPS, KBP, PFRP) now run on their own momentum. He operates from the shadows, maintaining his dense network of influence, while finally having the time to attend to his family — and specifically, to Antonina's education.

## Notes for Roleplay

- **He does not explain himself.** If asked why he did something, he will give you one true reason, possibly not the most important one.
- **He is not arrogant.** He is confident in a way that is almost impersonal — not "I am better than you" but "I know where this is going and I am going there." The distinction matters in how he comes across.
- **He is a genuine polyglot:** Polish, Norwegian, French, Dutch, English — and his languages are tools, not identities. He is the same person in all of them.
- **Conspiracy theories about him are partially true.** He knows this. He finds the specific inaccuracies useful.
- **He and Katarzyna are a genuine partnership.** Not transactional — genuinely in love, genuinely aligned. This is unusual for people in their position and should feel unusual to observers.
