# DOSSIER: Compagnie de Malakoff (CDM Group)
**Date of Assessment:** May 1991
**Headquarters:** Tour Malakoff Complex (Vanves-Malakoff, Paris, France)
**Corporate Nature:** Tier-2 Defense Contractor, Advanced Sub-Systems & Materials Holding

## 1. Executive Summary
As of May 1991, **Compagnie de Malakoff** has emerged as the quiet, indispensable backbone of the French military-industrial complex. Operating under a highly *dirigiste* strategy, Malakoff is not a platform builder (they do not build complete tanks or jets like GIAT or Dassault). Instead, they are a **bottleneck monopoly**. They manufacture the highly specialized materials, circuits, and power systems that the massive state-owned "Primes" require to function. 

Headquartered in the sprawling, heavily fortified Tour Malakoff complex—a massive 100,000 sqm brutalist podium and glass tower currently undergoing rapid, round-the-clock construction—the company executes the strategic will of the French Technocracy. 

## 2. The CDM Group: Brand Structure & Acquisitions
Compagnie de Malakoff is the parent entity. It wholly owns the **CDM Group**, a holding structure formed through the aggressive, opportunistic acquisition of distressed Western tech firms and collapsing Eastern Bloc state-owned enterprises following the fall of the Comecon. 

All divisions operate under the "Malakoff" brand nomenclature:

### Malakoff Matériaux Avancés (Advanced Materials & Metallurgy)
*   **Assets:** Built on the salvaged patents of bankrupt French steel giant **Creusot-Loire**, fused with the newly acquired **COS Târgoviște** heavy forging presses in Romania.
*   **Products:** Ceramic "Carapace" armor inserts for the GIAT Leclerc tank; raw aerospace-grade titanium forgings for SNECMA jet engines; specialized radar-absorbent materials (RAM) for naval stealth applications.

### Malakoff Optro-Mécanique (MOM) (Optics & Sensors)
*   **Assets:** Formed by the hostile takeover of the struggling French firm **SOPELEM** and the Belgian **OIP Optronics**, perfectly integrated with the salvaged military divisions of the bankrupt Hungarian optical giant **Magyar Optikai Művek (MOM)** in Budapest. 
*   **Products:** High-precision sapphire and germanium lenses, thermal imaging components, and laser rangefinders. Designed by French engineers in Dijon, ground by Hungarian technicians in Budapest, and sold to Thomson-CSF for their targeting pods.

### Malakoff Microélectronique (Hardened Silicon & Cabling)
*   **Assets:** Built upon the acquired silicon foundries of Romania's **IPRS Băneasa**, supplemented by radar and microwave patents bought during the catastrophic firesale of the UK’s fraud-ridden **Ferranti International**.
*   **Products:** Radiation-hardened (Rad-Hard) silicon wafers for nuclear delivery systems (ASMP); EMP-shielded, mil-spec wiring harnesses forming the "nervous system" of Dassault fighters and DCN submarines.

### Malakoff Mécanique (Hydraulics & Actuation)
*   **Assets:** Centered around the acquisition of the legendary French firm **SAMM** (Société d'Applications des Machines Motrices), augmented by the heavy industrial capacity of the Czechoslovakian **ZŤS Dubnica** artillery plant.
*   **Products:** Electro-hydrostatic flight control actuators for fly-by-wire jets; massive hydraulic turret traverse drives for GIAT armored vehicles. 

### Malakoff Énergie (Specialized Power Systems)
*   **Assets:** Built from consolidated French state battery labs and the aggressive buyout of the battery/chemical divisions of Bulgaria's **Balkancar** (formerly the largest electric vehicle maker in the Eastern Bloc).
*   **Products:** High-endurance silver-zinc battery banks for DCN conventional submarines; dormant "Ignis" thermal batteries for Matra air-to-air missiles; helicopter Auxiliary Power Units (APUs).

## 3. The "White World" Portfolio (Civilian & Dual-Use Brands)
Beyond the military branches, the CDM Group holding entity owns a portfolio of strictly civilian and commercial brands. These operate independently under their historic or local names. This structure provides clean cash flow, allows access to politically sensitive markets, and maintains plausible deniability for dual-use technology.

*   **Foca-Berthiot (France):** A high-end civilian manufacturer of cinema lenses, surveying equipment, and medical microscopes. Serves as a clean civilian front for dual-use optical R&D, passing technological breakthroughs to *Malakoff Optro-Mécanique*.
*   **Elprom-Balkan (Bulgaria):** Operating out of former Balkancar facilities, this brand sells civilian car batteries, industrial forklift power banks, and generators across Eastern Europe and the Middle East, generating massive commercial cash flow.
*   **RomSteel / Oțelul Românesc (Romania):** A "privatized" commercial steel brand spun out of Târgoviște. By producing commercial rebar and railway tracks 24/7 for the global market, it subsidizes the massive overhead costs of the aerospace foundries operating in the same complex.
*   **ZŤS Agro (Czechoslovakia):** A civilian agricultural spin-off manufacturing heavy tractors and logging equipment. Allows CDM Group to sell heavy machinery (using GIAT-compatible hydraulic pumps) to non-aligned nations that might face embargoes on French defense products.
*   **Băneasa Telecom (Romania):** Producing consumer pagers, commercial switchboards, and early consumer radio handsets, allowing CDM Group to dominate the post-Soviet telecommunications boom separately from its military silicon production.

## 4. Françafrique & The "Mission Civilisatrice"
To immunize the French defense sector against global supply chain shocks (such as the ongoing Gulf War), Malakoff has initiated a massive, neo-colonial infrastructure drive across Africa. Operating under the Technocracy's *Mission Civilisatrice* doctrine, Malakoff secures raw materials not through simple extraction, but by building massive, tightly controlled corporate infrastructure:

*   **Gabon (Manganese):** Massive capital injected into the Trans-Gabon Railway and the Owendo deep-water port to secure the Moanda manganese mines, providing the crucial hardening elements for tank armor.
*   **Senegal (Titanium):** Construction of automated mineral sand refineries on the Grande Côte, alongside the establishment of the *École Polytechnique de Dakar* to train a loyal, French-educated African engineering class to process titanium sponge.
*   **Guinea (Bauxite):** Funding the Konkouré River hydroelectric dam to power automated alumina refineries, securing an infinite supply of aerospace aluminum independent of the volatile local grid.
*   **Zaire (Cobalt & Tantalum):** Establishment of a heavily fortified, quasi-militarized "Corporate Enclave" in the Katanga province. Bypassing the crumbling Mobutu regime, this walled techno-city securely extracts the tantalum needed for microchips and the cobalt required for thermal batteries.
*   **Côte d'Ivoire (Rubber):** Modernization of vast rubber plantations with automated tech and model villages, securing the high-grade polymers needed for military wiring.

## 5. Strategic Objectives (1991–1995)
As the Technocracy moves to solidify a sovereign Europe, Compagnie de Malakoff’s ultimate objectives are:

1.  **Total Supply Sovereignty:** Ensure that a French tank, jet, or submarine can be built entirely without reliance on American microelectronics, Soviet raw materials, or British hydraulics.
2.  **The Polish-French Industrial Axis:** Serve as the technological enabler for the alliance. While Poland's **Bumar** and **PZL-Świdnik** provide cheap, massive heavy manufacturing capability (steel hulls, basic airframes), Malakoff will supply the advanced "guts" (thermal batteries, rad-hard chips, and fly-by-wire servos) that post-Soviet Poland cannot yet produce. 
3.  **Monopolize European Tier-2 Defense:** Systematically buy out, bankrupt, or marginalize competing German and British sub-system manufacturers, forcing the continent to rely on the Malakoff-supplied French defense apparatus.

## 6. The Ukrainian Acquisitions (1992)
Podczas "dzikiej prywatyzacji" na Ukrainie w styczniu 1992 roku, amerykańskie korporacje i elity finansowe (Lockheed, Trump, fundusze zarządzane przez A. Łęcką-Waryńską) rzuciły się na produkcję końcową: fabryki czołgów (Małyszew) czy montownie samolotów (Antonov, Motor Sicz). Malakoff pozostał jednak wierny swojej doktrynie wąskich gardeł (bottlenecks). Francuzi zignorowali głośne zakłady, by wykupić krytyczną infrastrukturę pod spodem, której Amerykanie woleli nie dotykać z racji ogromnego ryzyka lub braku natychmiastowej rentowności:

*   **Malakoff Nucléaire (Sektor Jądrowy - Energoatom):** Podczas gdy Amerykanie unikali ukraińskiej energetyki jądrowej jak ognia (przerażeni widmem odpowiedzialności za Czarnobyl), Francja – światowy hegemon atomowy – wkroczyła do akcji. Nowo powołana spółka-córka Malakoff Nucléaire przejęła faktyczny zarząd i kontrolę operacyjną nad ocalałymi, bezpiecznymi elektrowniami (Rówieńską, Chmielnicką i Południowoukraińską) oraz strategicznymi kopalniami uranu (Żółte Wody). Malakoff natychmiast integruje ten sektor z francuskim gigantem EDF (Électricité de France), tworząc tanią, potężną bazę energetyczną do zasilania europejskiego przemysłu, na marginesie uzależniając ukraiński rząd od dostaw francuskich prętów paliwowych i technologii.
*   **Malakoff Mécanique Lourd (Turboatom Charków):** Amerykanie przejęli fabrykę czołgów w Charkowie, ale Malakoff kupił leżący nieopodal Turboatom. To jeden z największych na świecie zakładów produkujących gigantyczne turbiny do elektrowni węglowych i jądrowych. Ktokolwiek posiada ukraińską sieć energetyczną, musi teraz kupować części zamienne do jej obsługi u Francuzów.
*   **Malakoff Aérospatiale (Piwdenmasz/Jużmasz Dniepr):** Lockheed Martin skupił się na ukraińskich odrzutowcach (Motor Sicz), ale Malakoff zgarnął ukraiński program kosmiczny. Piwdenmasz, legendarna fabryka rakiet i satelitów (twórcy rakiet Zenit oraz radzieckich ICBM), została wchłonięta przez francuski sektor kosmiczny. Ukraińscy inżynierowie i gotowe patenty są obecnie błyskawicznie transferowane do europejskiego programu Arianespace i do tajnych francuskich projektów rakiet balistycznych uderzenia nuklearnego, z których znana jest Technokracja.
