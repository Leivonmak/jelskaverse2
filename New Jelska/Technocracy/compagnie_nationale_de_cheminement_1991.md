# DOSSIER: Compagnie Nationale de Cheminement (CNC)
**Date of Assessment:** May 1991
**Corporate Nature:** State-Owned Enterprise (SOE)
**Primary Operations:** High-Risk Heavy Logistics, Pathfinding, Expeditionary Engineering, and Paramilitary Security.
**Colloquial Moniker:** "The Maersk of War Zones"

## 1. Executive Summary
The **Compagnie Nationale de Cheminement (CNC)** is a formidable, entirely independent French state-owned enterprise. While it maintains deep strategic ties with defense contractors like Compagnie de Malakoff, the CNC is not a subservient subsidiary. It is a sovereign entity operating far beyond the scope of a standard logistics provider. 

If a commercial shipping company refuses to enter a region due to civil war, lack of infrastructure, or piracy, the CNC is contracted. They provide "full-spectrum expeditionary logistics," acting simultaneously as a heavy cargo carrier, an expeditionary engineering corps, a private intelligence agency, and a paramilitary force capable of defending its own convoys against warlords or state actors.

## 2. Core Operational Pillars

### Heavy Logistics in High-Risk Zones
CNC operates a massive, unmarked fleet of ruggedized transport specifically built for austere and hostile environments.
*   **Aviation Fleet:** Utilizing a mix of heavy-lift aircraft (ex-Soviet Antonovs and Ilyushins acquired after 1989) and rugged French Transalls, capable of landing on unpaved dirt strips in the jungle under blackout conditions.
*   **Naval & Riverine:** Operating fortified, shallow-draft river barges deep in Zaire and heavy roll-on/roll-off (RoRo) ships capable of offloading heavy armor directly onto beaches without formal port infrastructure.
*   **Overland Convoys:** Massive, armored truck fleets designed to push through active war zones. 

### Pathfinding & Infrastructure Penetration
CNC specializes in moving cargo where no roads or maps exist. They employ elite "Pathfinder" teams—forward reconnaissance units tasked with surveying deep jungle, desert, or mountain routes. If a bridge is destroyed, CNC's embedded combat engineers rapidly construct pontoon bridges. If a jungle airstrip is overgrown, CNC heavy equipment clears it. They do not just navigate the world; they forcefully open it.

### Security & Intelligence (CNC-Securité)
Operating profitably in war zones requires teeth. CNC possesses its own dedicated paramilitary security wing, largely recruited from former French Foreign Legionnaires, paratroopers, and thoroughly vetted local assets.
*   **Convoy Protection:** Heavily armed escort vehicles, technicals, and airborne quick-reaction forces (QRF) ensure the cargo reaches its destination, aggressively deterring insurgencies or pirate cartels.
*   **Private Intelligence (HUMINT):** To safely navigate conflict zones, CNC maintains a sprawling human intelligence network across Françafrique, Eastern Europe, and the Middle East. They often anticipate coups or insurgent offensives before local governments do, allowing them to reroute shipments, extricate high-value clients, or leverage the instability.

## 3. The Client Base
Unlike Malakoff, which manufactures the tools of war, CNC is an outward-facing service provider. They sell access, mobility, and security.

*   **The French State:** CNC executes deniable logistical operations for French intelligence (DGSE) and the military, rapidly deploying materiel into regions where a formal French military deployment would be politically disastrous.
*   **The Defense Industry:** Compagnie de Malakoff relies heavily on CNC to securely extract highly sensitive strategic minerals (cobalt, coltan, titanium) out of deep Françafrique enclaves and transport them safely back to European foundries.
*   **External & Multinational Clients:** As a highly profitable SOE, CNC sells its "secure logistics" services to other friendly governments, multinational oil and mining corporations, and even the United Nations when blue-helmet forces require specialized heavy-lift capabilities in active combat zones.
