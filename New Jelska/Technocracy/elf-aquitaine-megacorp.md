# Elf Aquitaine — The Sovereign Megacorporation (1991 Redux)

In the real timeline, Elf Aquitaine in the early 1990s was a state-owned oil company that operated as an autonomous, deeply corrupt slush fund, ultimately leading to its privatization and the massive "Elf Affair" scandal in 1994. 

In the Technocracy's timeline, the trajectory is reversed. 

Rather than letting Elf slip into private hands and public scandal, Mitterrand and the Technocracy execute a **hostile state takeover** from within. In 1991, Elf Aquitaine is transformed from a corrupt semi-private entity into a hyper-efficient, legally untouchable instrument of French imperial dirigisme.

---

## 1. Legal Restructuring: The "SNCF Model"
In 1991, the French state executes a share buyout of the remaining minority stakes. Elf is legally restructured, losing its status as a standard commercial enterprise (*Société Anonyme*) and is effectively integrated into the state apparatus, akin to SNCF (the national railway). 

**The consequence:** Elf is no longer subject to standard corporate oversight or market shareholder demands. Its budget is tied to the state's strategic imperatives. It cannot go bankrupt, it cannot be acquired by foreign capital, and its internal audits are classified under national security laws. It is a megacorporation where the CEO is effectively a cabinet-level minister, and its operations are indistinguishable from French foreign policy.

## 2. The Corps des Mines Integration & The End of Pantouflage
In standard French governance, *pantouflage* is the practice of elite civil servants (like X-Mines engineers) leaving public service to take lucrative jobs in the private sector. 

Under the new Elf doctrine, **pantouflage is eliminated because Elf is no longer "the private sector."**
Elf Aquitaine becomes the official corporate deployment arm of the *Corps des Mines*. 
*   If you graduate from École Polytechnique and enter the Corps des Mines, your career path might take you to the Ministry of Industry, the nuclear sector (Framatome), or Elf Aquitaine. 
*   There is no conflict of interest because the interests are identical. An executive at Elf is a sworn state engineer. 
*   This instantly cleans up the vulgar corruption of the 1980s. The men running Elf in 1991 are not greedy businessmen looking for bonuses; they are ideological technocrats building an empire. 

*Note: This directly connects to Krzysztof Jelski. As an X-Mines graduate with years of experience at Royal Dutch Shell, Jelski is intimately familiar with both the academic doctrine of the Corps and the operational reality of global oil. He sees the new Elf as the perfect template for what he wants to build in Poland.*

## 3. The Modern "Mission Civilisatrice"
The old Françafrique model relied on Elf executives handing briefcases of cash to African dictators in exchange for oil rights. The Technocracy views this as crude, unstable, and embarrassing.

The new Elf Aquitaine Megacorporation operates like a late-20th-century East India Company, executing Mitterrand's civilized, structured integration of Africa:
*   **Infrastructure over Bribes:** Instead of paying a dictator $50 million to a Swiss bank account, Elf Aquitaine builds a $500 million deep-water port, a national power grid, and a telecommunications network. Elf owns the infrastructure, manages it, and integrates the African nation's economy directly into the European grid. 
*   **The Omani Connection:** Elf frequently partners with Omani sovereign wealth for these massive capital projects. Oman provides the financial backing (washed through Andorra), and Elf provides the engineering and corporate management.
*   **Intelligence Integration:** Elf's security division is formally merged with DGSE's Africa desk. Elf geological surveying planes are equipped with DGSE signals intelligence suites. Every Elf refinery is a sovereign French enclave, heavily guarded and fully capable of operating as a military logistics hub or a black site.

## 4. The Megacorporate Future
By integrating Elf completely into the state, France creates an economic weapon that the Anglo-American free market cannot compete with. American oil companies (Exxon, Chevron) have to worry about quarterly profits, shareholder dividends, and Congress. Elf Aquitaine only has to worry about the strategic 50-year plan of the Technocracy. 

If Elf needs to operate at a massive financial loss in a specific African country for five years just to drive American or Chinese competitors out of the region, the French state absorbs the loss. It is weaponized capitalism.

## 5. The Ukrainian Strategy (1992): Controlling the Veins
W styczniu 1992 roku, gdy Ukraina padła ofiarą masowej "dzikiej prywatyzacji", państwowe srebra rodowe (fabryki czołgów, samolotów, banki i hotele) zostały rozszarpane przez amerykański kapitał (Wall Street, fundusze powiązane z A. Łęcką-Waryńską, Trumpa i Lockheed Martin). 

Francuska Technokracja i Elf Aquitaine celowo zignorowały te głośne i medialne przetargi. Zamiast tego skupiły się wyłącznie na **krwiobiegu** energetycznym, którego Amerykanie nie potrafili zmonopolizować lub uznali za zbyt mało rentowny w krótkim terminie:

*   **Monopol Tranzytowy (UkrTransGaz / Rurociąg Przyjaźń):** Elf Aquitaine, poprzez sieć spółek joint-venture i gwarancji rządowych z Paryża, przejął operacyjną kontrolę nad ukraińskimi stacjami kompresorowymi oraz węzłami przesyłowymi gazu i ropy (w tym rurociągu Urengoj-Pomary-Użhorod i rurociągu "Przyjaźń"). Amerykańscy i ukraińscy oligarchowie (jak Ihor Kołomojski) mogą posiadać rafinerie w Krzemieńczuku, ale to Elf Aquitaine kontroluje **kurki i rury**, którymi surowiec z Rosji (od Lebiedzia) płynie do Europy. Daje to Paryżowi gigantyczną dźwignię geopolityczną, zwłaszcza na uzależnione od gazu Niemcy.
*   **Offshore na Morzu Czarnym:** Elf wykupił wyłączne prawa do poszukiwań i wydobycia gazu ziemnego na zachodnim szelfie Morza Czarnego i wokół Wyspy Wężowej, starannie omijając rosyjską strefę aneksji wokół Krymu. Francuzi budują tam niezależne, ufortyfikowane platformy wydobywcze, zabezpieczane przez francuską marynarkę wojenną pod płaszczykiem ochrony inwestycji.
